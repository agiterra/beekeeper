## The acceptance story

Brian and Andy work in one project from separate machines. Their agents see
overlapping work before pushing. The project's Runner procedure changes once;
both machines resolve that change and disclose what their current executions
actually use. Andy leaves. An authorized participant continues his slice from
a shared checkpoint, without a duplicate execution resuming later. CI reports
completion against the exact commit without a model polling. A delegated
reviewer resolves a structural question, records the decision and notifies
Brian while the work proceeds. Andy returns to a coherent completed slice and
its evidence.

This is the integrated test of the product, not a requirement to rebuild every
component before any useful improvement ships. Each increment below has a
usable surface and an executable acceptance case.
