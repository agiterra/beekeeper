---
name: write-report
description: "Report the change and the evidence needed to assess it."
---

Return the outcome, files or artifacts changed, exact revision, acceptance
checks and their observed results, deviations, remaining risks and next action.
Name failures and checks not run. Link to durable evidence available to the
recipient; disclose local-only artifacts. In a managed team use the supplied
assignment's report operation and verify acceptance. Prose alone is not that
operation, and an accepted report does not prove product acceptance.
