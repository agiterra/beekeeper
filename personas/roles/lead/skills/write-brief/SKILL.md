---
name: write-brief
description: "Make delegated work concrete and independently executable."
---

Give the worker the original goal, relevant project instructions, scope and
ownership, starting revision, dependencies, important constraints and acceptance
criteria. Include evidence rather than an advocate's summary. Name the available
capabilities, standing grants and reporting destination. Use actual session and
assignment references when the work belongs to a managed team. Carry facts
between workers explicitly; one worker cannot assume access to another's local
state. A handover preserves decisions, revision, artifacts and the next action.
