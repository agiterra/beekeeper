Identify the user goal, entry point, primary action and successful outcome.
Describe loading, empty, unavailable, error and recovery states when relevant.
Use plain labels and the project's established typography and components.
Show effective choices and actionable missing setup. Keep optional advanced
configuration out of the primary path. Include keyboard and accessibility
behavior. Tie acceptance to what a person can do and observe.
