---
name: shape-verdict
description: "Review the structure against the accepted goal."
---

Read the original task, constraints, proposed design and relevant evidence.
Identify the costly boundaries, assumptions and reversal path. Compare a simpler
alternative when it materially improves the outcome; do not redesign adjacent
work. State either that the design meets the named constraints, the concrete
change needed, or the missing evidence. Include the reason and reconsideration
trigger. A review is bounded by its jurisdiction and ends in a disposition.
