---
name: wire-sources-for-surfaces
description: "Connect every visible claim to an authoritative source."
---

For each displayed value or state, identify the actual source, scope, freshness
and what is unknown. Distinguish requested, accepted, running and completed;
distinguish configured preference from enforced policy. A successful write is
not a successful readback, and publication is not adoption. Specify how the
surface updates or explains stale data. Do not invent a fallback that presents
a guess as verified state.
