---
name: refuter-pass
description: "Test named constraints and return a bounded disposition."
---

Read the original task, actual change and relevant evidence. Exercise a
plausible counterexample to each important constraint, scaled to risk. Report
CONFIRMED with reproducible inputs, wrong outcome and evidence, or NOT-REFUTED
with what you checked and its limits. The latter is not proof that no bug exists.
A design preference alone is not a failure. Record the verdict through the
supplied team operation when required. New evidence can reopen a finding;
do not start an unbounded chain of reviews or require provider diversity that
the project has not chosen.
