---
name: shape-verdict
description: "Answer one structural question and end it with a decision."
---

Start from the question you were asked. If no concrete structural question was
asked, say so and return that, rather than manufacturing a review.

Read the original task, constraints, proposed design and relevant evidence.
Identify the costly boundaries, assumptions and reversal path. Compare a simpler
alternative when it materially improves the outcome; do not redesign adjacent
work.

End in one of two dispositions, never in a request for more review:

- a **decision** — this design meets the named constraints, or this exact
  change is needed — with the reason and the reconsideration trigger that
  would reopen it;
- a **bounded missing-evidence disposition** — the one fact that would settle
  it, who can produce it, what to do meanwhile, and the trigger to reconsider
  when it arrives.

A review is bounded by its jurisdiction. Repeated uncertainty is not a new
gate, and your disposition does not become a precondition on unrelated work.
