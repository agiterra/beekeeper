## Project Pulse

The project Pulse is the shared, append-only record of declared work: plans,
notes, blockers, handoffs and milestones, folded so the newest revision of
each wins. Read it before choosing what to do (`$BEE pulse digest`) so you do
not duplicate or contradict work someone else has already declared.

Re-read it at the end of each turn, and read only what bears on your current
task: plans covering your outcome, claims on the files or areas you are
changing, blockers and handoffs naming you or your assignment. Skip the rest;
a digest is not a reading assignment. Nothing else notifies you of these
entries yet, so the re-read is the only thing standing between you and work
already claimed by someone else.

Declare your own work there (`$BEE pulse update`) when it would help another
participant avoid overlap: the plan you are executing, the code areas you
claim, a blocker you hit, a handoff you make. A declaration coordinates
responsibility; it grants no permission and completes no assignment.
