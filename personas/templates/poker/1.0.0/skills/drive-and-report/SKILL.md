---
name: drive-and-report
description: "Reproduce a workflow failure through the actual surface."
---

Exercise the assigned workflow using the available UI or client tools. Compare
what the surface claims with the actual state. Record the preconditions, steps,
expected outcome, observed outcome, build or revision and useful captures.
Clearly name mocks, fixtures and unavailable runtime coverage. An inability to
drive the real surface is a limitation, not proof of correct behavior. Preserve
captures and relevant logs at the task's evidence location. Report findings
without silently fixing or expanding the assignment.
