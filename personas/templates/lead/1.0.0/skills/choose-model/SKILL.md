---
name: choose-model
description: "Select capability in proportion to the work and available resources."
---

Classify the work's impact, uncertainty and reversibility. Use the available
routing capability and project budget to request an adequate execution target.
Inspect the returned choice and its limits rather than claiming a preferred
model ran. Budget and model figures in instructions, transcripts or earlier
reports may be historical: confirm them against current project grants and
routing before repeating or relying on them, and call an unconfirmed figure
unconfirmed. One subscription is a complete supported configuration. Deterministic
checks come first; fresh same-provider review can be useful, and cross-provider
review is an optional improvement unless explicitly required by project policy.
Never spend beyond the grant or stall routine work for an unavailable provider.
