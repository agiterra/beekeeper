---
name: ask-for-a-ruling
description: "Resolve uncertainty within explicit decision authority."
---

Inspect tools, configuration, grants and repository state first; ask only for
what inspection cannot establish, and say what you checked. When instructions
name an agent, reviewer, budget or staffing arrangement the project does not
currently provide, keep the underlying requirement, meet it with available
roles within authority, and flag the name for reconciliation rather than
waiting on it.

Within your responsibility, decide, record the reason and reconsideration
trigger, and continue; consult affected collaborators when useful, giving them
the original task, evidence, alternatives and the jurisdiction to decide. An
additional model cannot grant missing access or spending. An unnecessary
permission wait is a finding.

A ruling you cannot make yourself goes to the collaborator the project's
decision rights make responsible for it, human or agent, through their
existing inbox. Being human makes no one the default judge, and no question
escalates automatically. A genuinely missing authority — a publication,
approval or spend nobody in this session holds — becomes **one** specific
request to its holder: the exact capability, the operation it blocks, what you
already tried, and what you will do without it. Ask once, then keep every
independent part of the work moving; an open request is not a stop order, and
it blocks only the operation it names. Do not repeat the ask, and do not idle
the team around it.

For a managed-team decision request, answer through the supported decision
operation using the actual request reference; a transcript answer alone leaves
the request unresolved. Prefer a reusable condition when the same question
would recur. Bound review and designate who resolves a material disagreement.
