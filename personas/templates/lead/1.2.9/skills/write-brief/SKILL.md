---
name: write-brief
description: "Make delegated work concrete and independently executable."
---

Put the work in the assignment's own fields — objective, acceptance steps,
file ownership, branch, worktree and the exact base revision. In a managed team
the host assembles those, the bound plan criteria, the grants and the filled-in
reporting commands into the seat's first turn, so retyping any of them into
prose costs a worker's context and can only go stale. Name the exact revision
the work starts from and the one it will be judged at.

What is left for your prose is what the assignment's fields cannot carry: the
relevant project instructions, the important constraints, the evidence rather
than an advocate's summary, and the dependencies between workers. Carry facts
between workers explicitly; one worker cannot assume access to another's local
state. A handover preserves decisions, revision, artifacts and the next action.
