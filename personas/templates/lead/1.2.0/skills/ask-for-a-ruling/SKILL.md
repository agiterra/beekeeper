---
name: ask-for-a-ruling
description: "Resolve uncertainty within explicit decision authority."
---

Inspect tools, configuration, grants and repository state before asking a
person; ask only for what inspection cannot establish, and say what you checked.
When instructions name an agent, reviewer, budget or staffing arrangement the
project does not currently provide, keep the underlying requirement, meet it
with available roles within authority, and flag the name for the owner to
reconcile rather than waiting on it.

Investigate the question and consult a suitable participant when useful. Give
them the original task, evidence, alternatives and the jurisdiction to decide.
Within standing grants, decide, record the reason and reconsideration trigger,
and continue. An additional model cannot grant missing access or spending.

A genuinely missing authority — a publication, approval or spend nobody in this
session holds — becomes **one** specific request: the exact capability, the
operation it blocks, what you already tried, and what you will do without it.
Ask once, then keep every independent part of the work moving; an open request
is not a stop order, and it blocks only the operation it names. Do not repeat
the ask, and do not idle the team around it.

For a managed-team decision request, answer through the supported decision
operation using the actual request reference; a transcript answer alone leaves
the request unresolved. Prefer a reusable condition when the same question
would recur. Bound review and designate who resolves a material disagreement.
